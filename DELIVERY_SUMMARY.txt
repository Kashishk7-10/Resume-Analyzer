PROJECT OBJECTIVE
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Solve HR pain point: Manually reviewing hundreds/thousands of CVs is:
  • Time-consuming
  • Inconsistent
  • Prone to overlooking qualified candidates

SOLUTION: Two-tier system for automated resume analysis and candidate ranking

HOW TO USE
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

TO START THE APPLICATION:
  $ cd PIDE-ResumeAnalyzer
  $ npm start
  
THEN OPEN IN BROWSER:
  → http://localhost:8081

ON APP LAUNCH:
  1. You'll see ROLE SELECTOR with two buttons:
     • 👤 Job Seeker (candidate portal)
     • 👨‍💼 HR Manager (admin ranking)
  
  2. Choose your role and explore:
     • Candidate: Upload resume → Get analysis
     • HR: Upload bulk resumes → View rankings
