## How to Use These Samples

### Testing with Copy-Paste
1. Open the App
2. Go to "Analyzer" tab
3. Copy the resume text above
4. Paste into the "Resume" field
5. Copy the job description
6. Paste into the "Job Description" field
7. Click "Analyze Resume"
8. Review the results

## Creating Your Own Test Data

### For Good Fit Resume
Include:
- Relevant technical skills matching job requirements
- Appropriate years of experience
- Similar job titles and responsibilities
- Technical certifications
- Relevant education background

### For Job Description
Ensure it matches:
- The technical skills in the resume
- Required experience level
- Industry and field
- Specific technologies and tools

### Tips for Testing
- Test with longer, more detailed resumes (200+ words)
- Use realistic job descriptions from actual job boards
- Try mismatched combinations to see "No Fit" results
- Test file uploads with real PDF/DOC files
- Verify confidence scores make sense for each match
